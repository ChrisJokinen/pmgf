# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
Critique proposals and evidence, not people. Harassment, discriminatory abuse,
threats, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may moderate contributions, request changes, or restrict
participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate,
documented where safe, and subject to review by an uninvolved maintainer when
one is available. The project does not promise independent moderation capacity.
