# Roadmap

This is an ordered research direction, not a delivery schedule or a statement
that the listed work is complete.

1. **Specify the deterministic core:** align schemas, charter, policy gates,
   coarse tool/resource identity, and simulated action vocabulary.
2. **Exercise durable invariants:** test refusal/deferral persistence,
   delegation inheritance, evidence-backed block resolution and re-evaluation,
   exact-plan approvals, expiry, per-decision single use, and boot-ID invalidation.
3. **Improve audit inspection:** verify linked provenance, schema evolution,
   chain checking, and explicit failure reporting without overstating integrity.
4. **Evaluate adversarial cases:** expand synthetic evidence, constraint
   conflicts, scope escapes, and task-equivalence fixtures; publish actual
   results and limitations.
5. **Specify operational handling:** document backup/restore, migration,
   retention choices, and administrator responsibilities.
6. **Reassess any scope expansion:** require a fresh threat model and explicit
   approval before considering authenticated identities, isolation, external
   trust anchors, or real adapters. None is a current capability or commitment.

Hard constraints remain binding throughout. Future research must not treat a
human override, utility score, or delegation path as an exception mechanism.
